Install ISU on Your Phone

Save ISU to your Home Screen so it opens like a regular app with one tap.

iPhone (Safari) 1. Open this page in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button at the bottom or top bar.
3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
Android (Chrome) 1. Open this page in Chrome.
2. Tap Install if prompted, or tap the ⋮ menu.
3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
